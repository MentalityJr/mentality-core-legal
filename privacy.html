Privacy
Policy

Last updated: 07/11/26

This policy explains what data Mentality Core stores, why, and how long we keep it.

1. What we store

  • Moderation: warnings, timeouts, kicks, bans, and case records. This includes the reason, the staff member who acted, and the time.
  • Applications and tickets: application answers, ticket channel details, and decisions.
  • Activity logs: quota entries (what you did, the date, attendance, and any screenshot or link you submitted), and leave-of-absence requests (dates and reason).
  • Auctions: bids and results, including your Discord name and ID.
  • Invite competitions: which invite link you joined through, when you joined, and who created that link.
  • Strikes and roles: strikes given for missed quotas or rule breaches, and role assignments.
  • Direct messages to the bot: the most recent 200 are kept so staff can respond.

The bot doesn't read or store ordinary messages from server channels.

2. Why we store it

To moderate the server fairly, run applications and tickets, track quotas and leave, run auctions and invite competitions, and answer questions from members.

3. Third parties

  • Discord hosts the server and the bot.
  • UnbelievaBoat is used to check and change balances for auctions.
  • Oracle Cloud hosts the bot.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this policy and the Terms of Service.

We don't sell or share your data for advertising.

4. How long we keep it

Moderation records are kept for as long as they're needed to run the server, then deleted on request. Quota and leave records are kept for the current season and then archived. Direct messages are limited to the most recent 200.

5. Your rights

You can ask what data we hold about you, ask for corrections, or ask for deletion by contacting us. We'll respond within a reasonable time.

6. Security

The bot runs on a private server with restricted access, and its secret keys are kept out of the code. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't guarantee absolute protection.

7. Children

The bot is intended for people who meet Discord's minimum age. If we learn that we've collected data from someone under that age, we'll delete it.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: Mentalityjrr@outlook.com
