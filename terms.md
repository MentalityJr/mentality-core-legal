# Terms of Service: Mentality Core

Last updated: 07/11/26

Mentality Core is a Discord bot operated by MentalityJr for the Mentality Studios community server. By adding the bot or using its commands, you agree to these terms.

## 1. What the bot does
Mentality Core provides server management tools: moderation actions and case records, applications and tickets, activity and leave-of-absence logs, invite competitions, auctions, giveaways, polls, role panels, and general utilities.

## 2. Who can use what
Commands are limited by role. Some are for staff, some for instructors or event hosters, and some are open to everyone. Trying to use a command you don't have access to will be refused.

## 3. Acceptable use
Don't try to break, abuse, spam, or exploit the bot. This includes automated requests, attempts to access other people's data, and using the bot to harass anyone.

## 4. Moderation and case records
Staff may warn, timeout, kick, or ban members under the server rules. Each action gets a case number. You can appeal through the appeal channel in the server.

## 5. Auctions and balances
Auctions use UnbelievaBoat balances. Bids are placed by pressing the button on an auction post. The highest bidder is charged once when the auction ends. The bot doesn't charge anyone during a bidding war.

## 6. Invite competitions
Invite competitions count people who join through a member's invite and are still in the server when the competition ends. Leaving the server removes the credit. Competition results are decided by the organiser, whose decision is final.

## 7. Availability
The bot is provided as-is. It may be offline for maintenance, updates, or reasons outside our control. We don't guarantee uninterrupted service.

## 8. Changes
We may change these terms or the bot's features at any time. Continued use after a change means you accept it.

## 9. Termination
We may stop the bot for any server or user that breaks these terms or the server rules.
